# Sample input

`ppe_demo_input_30s.mp4` (1920x1080, 30 fps, ~30 s, no audio) is a test input for the app.
It concatenates two stock clips, re-encoded to H.264:

| Segment | Source | License | Content (checked on preview frames) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–20 s | [Pexels video 13921040](https://www.pexels.com/video/13921040/) "Men Working on Construction Site" | [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) | Wide shot of a crew pouring concrete. Most workers wear hardhats, several wear no hi-vis vest, one wears a sun hat instead of a hardhat. |
| 20–30 s | [Pexels video 4281405](https://www.pexels.com/video/4281405/) "Men Working in a Warehouse" | [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) | High-angle shot of three workers in a warehouse aisle: no hardhats, no vests, two of them wear gloves. |

Authors could not be confirmed automatically (the Pexels pages block scripted access); see the links above for the author credit.

The clips are used only to illustrate the method. Detections produced on them are model outputs, not a judgement about the people shown.
